## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm an **AI Engineer and NLP Researcher**, a PhD candidate at **Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM)**, and a **Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS 2025)** recipient. I also teach Python programming and AI as an **Assistant Lecturer at the University of Technology, Iraq**.

My research focuses on **multilingual RAG dialogue systems for Arabic and Malay**, combining retrieval-augmented generation with **LoRA/PEFT fine-tuning of mT5**. I am interested in making language models useful for communities beyond English.

- 🔎 **Research:** multilingual retrieval, low-resource NLP, and dialogue systems.
- 🧩 **Adaptation:** LoRA, PEFT, mT5, and multilingual language models.
- 🤖 **Agents:** studying CrewAI, AutoGen, and LangGraph workflows.
- 🤝 **Collaboration:** research projects and freelance or remote AI/NLP work.
