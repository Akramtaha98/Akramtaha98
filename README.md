## Research meets engineering

I work at the intersection of **language models, information retrieval, and practical AI systems**. My research focuses on multilingual dialogue systems for **Arabic and Malay**, combining retrieval-augmented generation with parameter-efficient fine-tuning.

I am a PhD candidate in the Faculty of Information Science and Technology at **Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM)** and a **Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS 2025)** recipient. Alongside my research, I teach Python programming and AI as an Assistant Lecturer at the **University of Technology, Iraq**.

## Current focus

- **Multilingual RAG:** exploring retrieval and generation for Arabic and Malay dialogue systems.
- **Efficient adaptation:** combining LoRA/PEFT with mT5 fine-tuning for low-resource languages.
- **Agent workflows:** studying CrewAI, AutoGen, and LangGraph for multi-agent applications.
